# Who does what

The tool proposes; a person decides. Nothing is ordered, repriced or moved without a tick.

| Role | When | Does what | Sees |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Store manager** | Evening, before the ordering deadline | Ticks, edits (with a reason) or crosses tomorrow's order for each product | Order with range, stock and the reason |
| | Morning, before the store opens | Ticks or crosses small markdowns, and the surplus pickups (who collects what, when) | Short list, red first |
| **Area manager** | Morning, by the transfer approval hour | Approves or rejects the big markdowns (over a set share of the day's delivery) and every store-to-store move | Queue, biggest value first; which stores do worse than the chain on waste and empty shelves; the kill switch |
| **DC buying lead** | Evening to early morning | Plans tomorrow's buying from the all-store totals; decides whether to cut spot (mandi) buying where stock is long | Totals with range, price warnings, spot-first trimming advice |
| **Farmer-group coordinator** | Once a day | Passes the expected volumes to the farmer groups, as an estimate (not an order) | Volume per product per day, with range |

Limits that apply to everyone: confirmed farmer-group volume is never cut; a suggestion that
passes its deadline expires and nothing happens; if the tool fails, the usual process carries on.
